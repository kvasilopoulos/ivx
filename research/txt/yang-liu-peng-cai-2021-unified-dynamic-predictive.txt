Testing for predictability of asset returns has been a long history in economics and finance. Recently, based     Accepted January 2020
on a simple predictive regression, Kostakis, Magdalinos, and Stamatogiannis derived a Wald type test based
on the context of the extended instrumental variable (IVX) methodology for testing predictability of stock        KEYWORDS

of regressors being stationary or nearly integrated or unit root and the AR model for regressors with or
without an intercept. A simulation study confirms the good finite sample performance of the proposed
tests before illustrating their practical usefulness in analyzing real financial datasets.
