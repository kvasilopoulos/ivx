Enhancing the local power of IVX-based tests in predictive regressions

Matei Demetrescu 

Institute for Statistics and Econometrics, Christian-Albrechts-University of Kiel, Olshausenstr. 40-60, D-24118 Kiel, Germany

highlights

· In simple predictive models with persistent regressors, the extended instrumental variable method (IVX) stabilizes size, but loses power.
· Augmenting the test regression with the lagged dependent variable may seriously increase local power.
· The initial and the augmented IVX methods are equivalent under the null hypothesis of no predictability.
· The power gain of the augmented IVX method is maximal when the IVX method has smallest power.
