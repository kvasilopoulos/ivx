# Robust Econometric Inference

Drawing statistical inference on the coefficients of a short- or
long-horizon predictive regression with persistent regressors by using
the IVX method of Magdalinos and Phillips (2009) DOI:
10.1017/S0266466608090154 and Kostakis, Magdalinos and Stamatogiannis
(2015) DOI: 10.1093/rfs/hhu139.
